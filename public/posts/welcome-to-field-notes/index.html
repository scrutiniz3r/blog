Welcome to Field Notes

This is a newsletter in the oldest sense of the word: things worth noting, sent directly, on no schedule but the one dictated by having something to say.

There's no algorithm here. No engagement loop, no infinite scroll, no notification badge. If you want to keep reading, you subscribe to the RSS feed or you don't. That's the whole mechanism.

A few things you can expect:

  • Short essays, rarely more than a few hundred words
  • No paywall, no tiers, no upsell
  • Publishing when there's something worth publishing, not on a content calendar

If that sounds like your kind of thing, welcome. If not — no hard feelings, and thanks for stopping by.
