What are the advantages of having your parent work in LIC?

Is it the stability or benefits? actually neither, it’s auto enrolment into policies which you study about afterwards. 

One such policy that i got aquatinted with is, as you guessed it, Term insurance.

Term, end of a term, death are the kind of things one may think twice before posting but when you think about the philosophy of writing a blog- this is something that’ll live post your death however only those who search for it will see it. 

Term insurance is an insurance of your death. You pay like ₹2500 per year for like 15 years and if you die within those 15 years, your nominee gets 50L. 
If you live, you get nothing. 

So you are paying this every year expecting that you should not be able to use it, which is quite ironical in this economy. (Or any economy that is)

But here an alternate way to look at it, you simply start living more! 
You take more risks, the amusement park rides get more amusing and worry free! 
You are not worried about leaving earth with such a low savings on your name- your coffin can say “Died, but atleast i’m paying my off my cryogenic coffin”
