Like mkbhd would say “I have been using the iphone for more than one month now”,
I actually got this as a gift and one of the rare times I was dumbfounded when i unboxed something. 

![Screenshot 2026 09 23 012147](/images/screenshot-2026-09-23-012147-mud3byb4.png)

This is my first time long-terming an iphone, so this would be a review on ios as well iphone 12 mini.

First and foremost, let’s throw battery out of the picture as this is my secondary phone and I mostly use it indoors, so it’s mostly plugged in but if you want to get into the details- battery health is 78% and Screen on time you can stretch is around 3 hours without mobile data. 

![IMG 0438](/images/img-0438-mud3cnxz.png)

Now that the achilles heel is out of the way, I must say- coming from 8+ years of android, ios is pretty smooth and refined. 
If I were to draw parallels, ios is PVR and android is Cinepolis. (PVR Lulu vs Cinepolis MOT for tvm people)

Cinepolis won’t make you feel like you don’t belong there, it gives you shit-ton of popcorn for the price you pay and the guardrails found doesn’t seem too restrictive. 

PVR, you know PVR. 

Let me list the things I love about the entire experience. 

1. Notification and typing sounds and haptics (in fact, i’m typing this blog in iphone for that very reason)
2. Forever one-hand mode
3. Good for quick snaps
4. ios animations
5. Disappears in my pocket
6. Apple music UI
7. “Not boring” apps
8. Super fun to play vertical games (crossy roads and saw blades)
9. ipod like utility with earphones (apple wired lighting earphones so goood)
10. Still gets latest os update unlike my 3 year old NP 2.
11. Attention it gets from others
12. Direct sticker making from photos in whatsapp

![IMG 0437](/images/img-0437-mud37ib4.png)

A lot of cons would be just me not adapting enough to ios, kindly keep my 1 month timeline in mind (:

Things that aren’t so good are
1. Battery ofc
2. Lightning type
3. OIS issue (prolly specific to mine)
4. Backspacing, autocorrelation and navigating while typing compare to gboard.(Finally figured out navigation thanks to a friend) 
5. Cannot install vanced. 
6. Some basic settings I had to google to find out to change like alarm sound!? How to enable explicit in apple music etc. 

I kinda find myself ditching my main phone when i’m tired of the constant notifications and unwinding with saw blades and Mohd. Rafi songs during my routine coffee timings.
